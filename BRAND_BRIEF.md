# Brand brief (for designs)

Status: no name, logo or colours chosen yet. First design task: make a design system (colours, type, spacing, logo direction) as a Claude Design System artifact, then use it for everything.

- Who we design for: office managers and owners of small US medical practices (1 to 10 doctors), and billing companies. Busy, careful, wary of anything that looks risky with patient data.
- What they must feel: "this gets my money back and is safe". Trustworthy, clear, calm, professional; not flashy, not "AI hype".
- Main message: "We appeal your denied claims. You pay only when we win."
- Proof to show (once real): dollars recovered, win rate, days to payment, HIPAA agreement signed with every practice.
- Never show: real patient data, real claim files, fake testimonials or made-up results presented as real.
- First pieces to design: design system; one-page website; 5-slide pitch deck; one-page practice flyer; email signature; LinkedIn banner.
