How it works

  1. 1

    Send us your denials

    Upload the denial letter, explanation of benefits (EOB) or remittance file through our secure upload. We find the reason for each denial and note its appeal deadline.

  2. 2

    We write the appeal, and a person checks it

    AI drafts the appeal from your records and the insurer’s own rules. A trained person on our team checks every fact against your records, then sends it. If a record is missing, we ask you for it first.

  3. 3

    The insurer pays you

    When an appeal wins, the insurer pays your practice directly. Then we send you our bill for that appeal.

Pricing

You pay only when we win. If an appeal loses, you owe us nothing for it.

How we keep patient data safe

When you send us patient records, this is how we look after them.

  • A HIPAA agreement comes first

    We sign a HIPAA business associate agreement (BAA) with your practice before you send us a single claim.

  • Patient data stays in our claims system

    Claims and records are kept in our secure claims system. Any vendor that handles them, such as the AI service that drafts appeals, signs a BAA with us first. We never put them in email, messaging apps or our sales tools.

  • A secure upload, never email

    You send records through our secure upload. We will never ask you to email patient details.

  • Only what the insurer needs

    A trained person checks every appeal before it goes out. Each one carries only the patient details the insurer needs.
