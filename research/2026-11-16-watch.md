## Changes and baseline

1. Medicare amount in controversy for 2027 (CMS-4213-N)
   - What: for appeals filed on or after 1 Jan 2027, an ALJ hearing (level 3) needs at least US$200 in dispute (no change) and federal district court (level 5) needs US$2,000 (up from US$1,960 in 2026). Same amounts for Parts A, B, C and D.
   - Links: Federal Register, 16 Sep 2026, Vol. 91, pages 58681 to 58682, https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-09-16/pdf/2026-19016.pdf ; First Coast notice dated 5 Oct 2026, https://medicare.fcso.com/appeals/changes-amount-controversy-aic-appeals-2027 . Read 16 Nov 2026.

2. WISeR prior authorization model (Original Medicare, six states)
   - What: runs 1 Jan 2026 to 31 Dec 2031 in Arizona, New Jersey, Ohio, Oklahoma, Texas and Washington. A claim sent without prior authorization gets pre-payment review for dates of service from 15 Jan 2026. A denial still counts as an initial determination with normal appeal rights under 42 CFR part 405, subpart I. CMS FAQ last modified 22 Sep 2026.
   - Links: https://www.cms.gov/priorities/innovation/files/document/wiser-model-frequently-asked-questions ; HCC Buddy summary, 8 Jul 2026 (third party, not CMS), https://hccbuddy.com/news/wiser-model-ai-prior-authorization-2026 . Read 16 Nov 2026.

3. WISeR repeal effort
   - What: per HCC Buddy (8 Jul 2026), GAO decision B-337994 (12 May 2026) found the WISeR notice is a rule under the Congressional Review Act, and S.J.Res.192, a resolution to overturn it, was placed on the Senate calendar on 9 Jun 2026 without passing. Not yet checked on Congress.gov, so its status today is unknown.
   - Link: https://hccbuddy.com/news/wiser-model-ai-prior-authorization-2026 . Read 16 Nov 2026.

## What it means for us (our estimate)

- Item 1: the court threshold rises by US$40, which almost never touches small practice claims; the ALJ threshold is unchanged. The fake claims and appeal rules need no change.
- Item 2: WISeR denials in those six states are appealable like any other Medicare denial, so practices there may have more denials to appeal in 2026 and later. Worth one line in the sales pitch for practices in those states.
