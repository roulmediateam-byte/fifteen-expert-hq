|---|---|---|---|
| Federal Register, Medicare appeals notices | https://www.federalregister.gov/documents/search?conditions%5Bterm%5D=%22Medicare+Appeals%22 | Yearly amount in controversy notice and any rule that changes appeal levels or deadlines. | human:melvin |
| CMS, Medicare fee-for-service appeals | https://www.cms.gov/medicare/appeals-grievances/fee-for-service | The five appeal levels and their deadlines, as CMS states them. | human:melvin |
| CMS, third level of appeal (OMHA) | https://www.cms.gov/medicare/appeals-grievances/fee-for-service/third-level-appeal | ALJ hearing threshold and filing rules. | human:melvin |
