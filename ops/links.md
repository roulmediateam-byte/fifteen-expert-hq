# Links

The single list of everything the company has: documents, designs, files, links, accounts and tools. HQ shows it as cards on its Everything tab. One row each, newest at the bottom. Never add a link that opens patient data, and never a password or key.

- Adding a row: open a pull request, or use HQ's "Add something" form, which opens a `"File: <name>"` Issue for the chief of staff. The chief of staff also adds a row for every File: Issue it files.
- Area: one of Company and strategy, YC, Brand and design, Product and engineering, Sales, Legal and finance, Ops, Other projects.
- Status: active (in use), draft (being worked on, or waiting for a review), planned (not live yet), archived (kept, no longer used).
- Claude artifacts stay private until someone shares them, so a claude.ai link here opens only for the people it is shared with.
